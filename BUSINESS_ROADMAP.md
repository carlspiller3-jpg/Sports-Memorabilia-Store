**Objective:** Scale from 600 leads to £1.1M Turnover using Hormozi-led systems.

## 1. The Grand Slam Offer (The Value Equation)
*To charge premium prices, we must maximize the perceived value while minimizing the client's effort.*

| Variable | Action | Implementation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dream Outcome** | Own a "Historical Asset" | Sell the *Asset Class* of the item (EEAT), not just a signed shirt. |
| **Likelihood of Success** | 100% Authenticity Guarantee | Use the digital NFC certificate as the primary proof of value. |
| **Time Delay** | Instant Delivery / Gratification | Next-day secure courier as standard. |
| **Effort & Sacrifice** | Zero Risk for the Buyer | Lifetime Money-Back Guarantee and Museum-Grade Framing included. |

## 2. Content Engine: "Document, Don't Create"
*You don't need athlete footage. You need to show the "Cost of Production" to justify the high price.*

### The "Provenance Line" Video Strategy (Short-form)
Film the birth of every product in 4 stages:
1.  **The Raw Material:** Close-up of the unattached number or glove.
2.  **The Signature:** Pen meeting fabric. Macro focus.
3.  **The Craft:** The mounting and framing process in the workshop.
4.  **The NFC Tagging:** The phone tapping the item and the certificate appearing.

**The Narrative:** "We don't buy memorabilia. We archive sports history. This is how a raw signature becomes a museum asset."

## 3. Monetizing the 600 (Klaviyo Playbook)
*You have 600 leads. Treat them like a private investment club, not a spam list.*

### Phase A: The "Founder's Drop" (Days 1-7)
*   **Email 1 (The Why):** Direct-response, plain text. "I'm Carl. I started this because I was tired of paper certificates and fake signatures. We're live."
*   **Email 2 (The How):** Show the "Provenance Line" video. Explain the NFC tech.
*   **Email 3 (The Scarcity):** "Only 12 Bruno gloves left. Once they're gone, the archive closes for restock."

### Phase B: Automated Flows
*   **Abandoned Cart:** 1-hour delay. Focus on the "Asset Value" (e.g., "Don't let this piece of history go to someone else's collection").
*   **Post-Purchase:** Request a video testimonial of the unboxing. **Offer £50 store credit for a high-quality unboxing video.**
