The Standard

Sports Memorabilia Store provides fully authenticated, framed sports memorabilia. All items are delivered in premium packaging and include digital verification.

1. Framing

Items are mounted in solid black wooden frames with white acid free mounts and custom metallic plaques.

2. Packaging

Every frame is delivered in a matte navy lid box with gold foil branding and internal protective lining.

3. Verification

Each item contains an NFC chip linked to a digital Certificate of Authenticity. We are led by a licensed FIFA agent.
