THE PROBLEM

The Market is Broken.

1. THE FAKE PANDEMIC

The industry is absolutely riddled with fakes. Trust is at an all time low because buyers have zero proof that what they're paying for is the real deal.

You drop hundreds of pounds on a signed shirt, and it turns up in a basic plastic mailer. It completely kills the magic. The unboxing should feel like a reward, not an afterthought.

3. STUCK IN THE PAST

You're forced to choose between the chaos of eBay or high street shops that haven't changed since the 90s. There's no serious brand building for the modern collector.
