Investment and Rights

The partnership includes a commercial rights fee of one hundred and fifty thousand pounds per season. This includes exclusivity for the brand across all signed memorabilia categories. The partnership also includes full rights to match worn shirts from every first team fixture throughout the campaign.

Signature Commitment

The inventory plan includes two hundred and fifty signatures from each player and ambassador per season. Our logistics team handles all preparation and setup. This keeps signing sessions brief and ensures the process fits seamlessly into the training schedule.

Matchday Hospitality

The partnership includes matchday hospitality for four personnel at three games per season. We look forward to utilizing a corporate box for these fixtures, excluding only the Glasgow Derby and any major finals.

Media Rights

We will generate our own content during signing sessions for marketing and authentication purposes. We also propose announcing the partnership via the official club social channels and monthly email newsletters to reach the supporter base directly.
