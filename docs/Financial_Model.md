### 2. Investment Structure: Convertible Loan Note

*   **Principal Investment:** £400,000
*   **Investment Vehicle:** Convertible Loan Note (CLN)
*   **Valuation Cap:** £2,000,000
*   **Primary Use of Funds:** Execution of the anchor club partnership (£180k) and seeding the initial athlete signing pipeline.

---

### 3. Execution Milestones (The Runway)

*   **Month 1 to 3:** Complete the primary club partnership acquisition. Launch the first 10 individual athlete signing cycles.
*   **Month 4 to 6:** Finalize the moment detection engine to automate inventory acquisition. Reach a consistent sales velocity of 5 units per day.
*   **Month 9:** Achieve the £750k revenue milestone. Stabilize the 40 athlete pipeline.
*   **Month 12:** Hit £1.1M total annual revenue. Maintain a 30% plus EBITDA margin.

---

### 4. Expansion Plan (24 Months and Beyond)

*   **Market Dominance:** Scale from 40 to 100 athlete signing cycles per year by targeting European league stars.
*   **Asset Management:** Launch a proprietary title transfer ledger to capture a percentage of all secondary market transactions for verified assets.
*   **Institutional Sales:** Expand the corporate gifting division into international law firms and wealth management offices.
*   **Logistics Scaling:** Move to a centralized distribution and framing center to reduce unit COGS through volume purchasing of raw materials.
